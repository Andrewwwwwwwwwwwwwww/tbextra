# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-21

First release.

- Reskin any Traveler's Backpack. The pack you get back is the same backpack, so its
  ability, tier, upgrades and contents are untouched - only its name and model change.
  A bookshelf backpack reskinned as a Firewatch still does what a bookshelf backpack does,
  and shift still shows the ability as usual.
- **Firewatch Backpack** - any backpack, eight campfires and an empty bundle.
- **Trapper's Backpack** - any backpack, eight iron bars and an empty bundle.
- Both recipes appear in the recipe book.
- Reskinning is one-way. For a pack with no ability, reskin a plain backpack.
- Reskinned packs get a collision shape matching their model rather than the shape of the
  backpack they were crafted from.
